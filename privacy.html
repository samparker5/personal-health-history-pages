Privacy Policy

This policy covers the current public policy website and the intended practices of my personal health-history application, which is still in development.

Current status. The application does not yet operate a live SMART on FHIR connection. This public website contains informational pages only, does not request healthcare login credentials, and does not accept patient records.

1. Information I intend to access

After my explicit authorization with a participating healthcare provider, the planned application may retrieve my available patient demographics, laboratory observations, diagnostic reports, medications, conditions, allergies, vital signs, procedures, encounters, immunizations, and clinical documentation. Actual available information depends on the provider and its supported access.

2. Purposes of use

I intend to organize my own records into a longitudinal medical history, analyze compatible lab measurements over time, search documents, and generate source-supported informational summaries. The app is intended to request read-only access and not change provider records.

3. Storage and retention

The planned application will store retrieved clinical information locally on my own device, rather than on this public website or in the GitHub repository. Data deletion, retention, and security controls are being designed and will be documented before real medical information is processed. Providers separately retain their records under their own policies.

4. Sharing and third parties

This GitHub Pages website does not receive or publish my medical records. The planned application is not intended to sell health information, serve advertisements, or make medical records available to unrelated parties. Patient authentication will be handled by the healthcare provider. Any future use of external AI or data-processing services will require an updated policy describing the service and data flows before records are sent.

5. Website hosting

This static information website contains no intentionally installed tracking pixels or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary technical request information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Safeguards and limitations

The intended application will use patient-authorized SMART on FHIR communication and appropriate local security practices. No method of storage or communication can be guaranteed completely secure. Security safeguards have not yet been fully implemented or evaluated.

7. Updates and contact

This policy will be updated as implementation progresses and before any material change in how medical information is handled. For questions about the policy, consult the public project repository, without posting personal health data or credentials.

Terms and Conditions

Personal Health History is a private, noncommercial application under development for its developer's exclusive personal use.

Development status. The application is not yet operational and is not offered as a public service. Features described here are planned rather than available.

1. Intended use

I am developing this application solely to access, organize, and understand my own health records. It is not intended for other patients, commercial distribution, or use by healthcare organizations.

2. Authorized access

The proposed application will request access only after I authorize it through my healthcare provider's SMART on FHIR login process. The integration is intended to be read-only. It will not create, change, or delete information in a provider's medical record system. Data availability and permissions depend on the participating provider.

3. Informational limitations

Any future timelines, charts, summaries, and analyses are for personal information and organization, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. They are not a substitute for consulting qualified healthcare professionals. For emergencies, seek appropriate emergency assistance.

4. Accuracy and completeness

Medical records may be incomplete, delayed, inconsistent, or unavailable. Automated processes can introduce errors or omit information. The intended application will retain source context and indicate uncertainty where possible but will not guarantee complete or clinically accurate results.

5. Security and privacy

The intended design uses local storage for my medical data. This public website provides policy information only and does not accept or store medical records. See the Privacy Policy for current status and planned data handling.

6. Changes

These terms may change as the project is built. Material changes to actual data-handling practices will be reflected in updated public policies before being implemented.

7. Questions

For questions about these terms, visit the public project repository. Do not share medical information or sensitive credentials through public GitHub discussions.
